# The story of the founding

> This is a work of fiction, written for fun. The story here is how the kingdom within the fiction came to be. It does not tell of real events or people.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](STORY.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

This story retells the [Founding Declaration and the Basic Law](founding.en.md) in plain words. Where they differ, the Declaration and the Basic Law apply.

## How the kingdom that is nowhere began

A king was counting the mistakes in the texts he had written.
A mistake that is erased can no longer be seen.
But an erased mistake becomes a second mistake (Article 5 of the Basic Law).
The king decided not to erase his mistakes, and to line them up with dates.
The mistakes he lined up formed a small island around his bookshelf.

When he tried to name the island, the king noticed something.
This kingdom is nowhere. He owns no land that appears on any map.
So he named the kingdom Nusquama, a name made from the Latin nusquam ("nowhere").
He named himself Nemo, Latin for "no one".

On 7 November 2026 the king declared the founding.
The next day he recorded his first mistake in the errata. It was a mistake about where the kingdom's name came from.
And so the kingdom's first territory was a mistake about its own name.

## Why mistakes are the domain

A mistake hidden as a shame will happen again.
A mistake kept as a record spares the next reader from stumbling at the same place.
So this kingdom takes pride not in how few mistakes it makes, but in how honestly it keeps the record (Founding Declaration).
The domain grows because the king has made that many mistakes.
It also grows because he has corrected that many.

## What comes next

The king will make more mistakes.
Each time, he adds a row to the [King's errata](ERRATA.en.md) and the domain grows by one parcel.
Anyone who has publicly corrected one of their own mistakes can join the [Ordo Erratorum](KNIGHTS.en.md).
