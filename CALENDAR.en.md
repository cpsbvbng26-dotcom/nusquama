# Calendar and days of the kingdom

> This is a work of fiction, written for fun. The days here are anniversaries within the fiction. They are not real public holidays, and they give no one a holiday or a duty.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](CALENDAR.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

The calendar of Nusquama uses the same dates as the calendar of Japan.

| Day | Name | Origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 November | Founding Day | On this day in 2026, Nemo I, Knight-King, declared the founding |
| 8 November | Day of Errata | On this day in 2026, the King's errata were opened and the first mistake recorded |

On these days the King reads again the mistakes he admitted that year.
No knight, and no one else, is required to do anything.
