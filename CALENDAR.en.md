# Calendar and days of the kingdom

> This is a work of fiction, written for fun. The days here are anniversaries within the fiction. They are not real public holidays, and they give no one a holiday or a duty.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](CALENDAR.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

The calendar of Nusquama uses the same dates as the calendar of Japan.

| Day | Name | Origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 November | Founding Day | On this day in 2026, Nemo I, Knight-King, declared the founding |
| 8 November | Day of Errata | On this day in 2026, the King's errata were opened and the first mistake recorded |

On these days the King reads again the King's own mistakes admitted that year.
No knight, and no one else, is required to do anything.

## Importing the calendar

The days are also served as an [iCalendar file](site/calendar.ics). Imported into a calendar app, it shows the days every year.
They come in as all-day days that do not block time, and each says that it is an anniversary within the fiction and not a real public holiday.

## Spending the days

These are suggestions from the King, not rules.

- Founding Day: read the Founding Declaration and the Ten Precepts again.
- Day of Errata: read one of your own texts again. If you find a mistake, correct it with a date.

## Adding a day

Days are added by the King ([Institutions](INSTITUTIONS.en.md), "How decisions are made").

- Only days on which something happened in this kingdom are added.
- Real public holidays and the birthdays or death days of real people are not made days of the kingdom.
- When a day is added, a row is added to this table and to the timeline, so that it appears in the gazette.
- Adding a day gives no one a holiday or a duty.
