# Words of the kingdom

> This is a work of fiction, written for fun. The words here are used within the fictional kingdom. They are not terms of any real state or organisation.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](LEXICON.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

## Vocabulary

These are the Latin words used in the names and institutions of the kingdom.
Their meanings come from general knowledge and have not been checked in a dictionary (Precept 1).
The Japanese page also gives a reading in kana, as a rough guide to the classical pronunciation.
If a mistake is found, it is recorded in the [King's errata](ERRATA.en.md).

## The Founding Declaration in Latin

This is the [Founding Declaration](founding.en.md) put into Latin by the King.
The Japanese declaration governs; this is the King's own composition. If a grammatical mistake is found, it is recorded in the King's errata.
Two sentences of the declaration are not carried over: the one on Thomas More and the one stating what the name means. Both contain mistakes, as entries 1 and 3 of the [King's errata](ERRATA.en.md) record.

Est regnum quod nusquam est. Nomen ei Nusquama.

In regno quod nusquam est, rex est qui nemo est. Nomen ei Nemo.

Rex Eques Nemo Primus populum non habet. Unum tantum regit, se ipsum.

Hic rex gladium non stringit. Arma regis probationes sunt; scutum eius tabula errorum est.

Magnitudo regis non paucitate errorum aestimatur, sed fide tabularum quae errores non delent.

Regnum uno rege finitur; heres non constituitur. Solus enim qui solus stat se ipsum solus corrigere potest.

Hic Regnum Nusquamae conditum esse declaro.

Nemo I, Rex Eques, die VII mensis Novembris anno MMXXVI.
