# Gazette

> This is a work of fiction, written for fun. This gazette lists the proclamations of the King within the fiction. It is not a real official gazette.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](GAZETTE.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

The events of the kingdom are listed newest first.
They come from the timeline on the top page, the [King's errata](ERRATA.en.md) and the [roll of knights](KNIGHTS.en.md). The gazette is made from them, not written by hand.
New entries can also be read in the feed (gazette.xml).
