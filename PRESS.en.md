# For anyone introducing Nusquama

> This is a work of fiction, written for fun. Nusquama is not a state, and the knightly names it gives are not decorations, honours or ranks of knighthood, and have no legal effect.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](PRESS.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

Text and numbers for anyone introducing Nusquama in an article or a post are gathered here.

## Short introductions

- In a phrase: a kingdom that is nowhere, whose territory is its mistakes.
- In a sentence: The Knightly Kingdom of Nusquama is a playful fiction whose domain grows each time its king records one of the King's own mistakes in the errata.
- In a paragraph: Nusquama is a fictional kingdom named from the Latin nusquam ("nowhere"). Its only king, Nemo I, Knight-King, records the King's own mistakes with dates and never erases them. The number of recorded mistakes is the kingdom's domain. A person who has publicly corrected one of their own mistakes can be put on the roll of knights, free of charge, only if they meet the conditions of the Rules on Knighting. Applications are paused until 00:00 on 2026-11-22 (Japan time). After that, only people aged 16 or over who live in Japan and are in Japan when they apply may apply. People who hold Italian nationality, or the nationality of a country that restricts accepting foreign titles, may not apply. The titles have no legal effect, and no money changes hands.

## For the micronation community

Text for introducing Nusquama in English on micronation wikis and forums. Its first sentence says that Nusquama is fiction.

This is a work of fiction, written for entertainment. The Knightly Kingdom of Nusquama (Regnum Nusquamae) is a fictional micronation whose founding was declared on 7 November 2026. It is not a state: it claims no territory and no sovereignty, and it seeks no recognition as a state. Its only king, Nemo I, Knight-King, records the King's own mistakes in the errata with dates and never erases them. Each recorded mistake adds one parcel to the kingdom's domain. Anyone who has publicly corrected one of their own mistakes can be put on the roll of knights free of charge, under the Rules on Knighting. From 2026-11-22, only people who live in Japan and are in Japan when they apply may apply. People who hold Italian nationality may not apply, even if they also hold another nationality, and nor may people who hold the nationality of a country that restricts accepting foreign titles. Knighting presumes a person of good sense who treats the title as play. The titles have no legal effect and are used only within the kingdom. Exchanges with other nations are limited to greetings and links to each other's pages: Nusquama makes no treaties, gives no titles in exchange, and handles no money.

This table matches English terms common in the micronation community with what Nusquama calls the same thing.
Where Nusquama has no such thing, the table says "none".
An introduction in English uses the terms in the second column.

| --- | --- | --- |
| micronation | fictional micronation | It does not claim to be a state and seeks no recognition as one |
| head of state, monarch | the King (Nemo I, Knight-King) | The King has no subjects; the one person the King rules is the King alone |
| declaration of independence | Founding Declaration | It declares independence from no country |
| constitution | Basic Law | It has no legal effect |
| citizens, citizenship | none | Knights are not citizens; no nationality is given |
| territorial claim | domain | No land is claimed; the domain is the number of mistakes in the King's errata |
| intermicronational relations, diplomacy | exchange | Limited to greetings and links to each other's pages |
| treaty, mutual recognition, embassy | none | No treaty or document of recognition is made, and no ambassador is appointed |
| honours, order of chivalry | roll of knights (Ordo Erratorum) | There is one rank, knight, and no medal, badge or other object is made |
| official gazette | Gazette | Not a real official gazette; made from the timeline, the errata and the roll |
| national day | Founding Day | Not a real public holiday; it gives no one a holiday or a duty |
