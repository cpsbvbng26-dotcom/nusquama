# Roll of the Order of Errata (Ordo Erratorum)

> This is a work of fiction, written for fun. The titles of those named here have no legal effect and no real honour.
> The name of knight is not a decoration, honour or rank of knighthood of any country.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](KNIGHTS.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

The knights are listed in the order of their knighting. There is only one rank, "knight", with nothing above or below it (Basic Law, Article 7).
Names are given as GitHub account names, with no real name or affiliation.
A knight may ask at any time to be taken off the roll ([Rules on Knighting](policies/knighting.en.md)).
Because Nusquama is run by one individual, its barriers to participation are set extremely high.
The conditions are in [Rules on Knighting](policies/knighting.en.md), "Barriers to participation".

## How entries are recorded

On knighting, one row is added to the table. The issue of the [gazette](GAZETTE.en.md) is made from that row.
From 2026-12-15, at most 1 person is knighted in a year (1 January to 31 December, Japan time). The table also has at most one row for each year of the day of knighting. The checks count the rows of the table to confirm this.
From 2026-12-15, at most 1 person is knighted in 2 years. No row is added until 2 years, not counting the day of knighting of the previous row, have passed. The checks confirm this from the days of knighting in the rows of the table ([Rules on Knighting](policies/knighting.en.md), "Barriers to participation (continued)").

- No.: continues from 1 with no gaps.
- Knight: the GitHub account name.
- Knighted: the day the King decided on the knighting (Japan time). The row is added on that same day.
- Mistake corrected: a summary of what was wrong and how it was corrected, with a link to the correction.
- Application: a link to the application issue.

Gazette issues are numbered in the order rows are added to the timeline and the roll. As the day of knighting is the day the row is added, an older date never comes after a newer one.
Decided by Claude on 2026-11-12, after the dry run of knighting found that a timeline row dated after the day of the decision puts the issues out of date order.

The gazette proclaims "Account-name is knighted as knight no. 1".
The issue for the first knight has the same form as every other. The Basic Law sets no ranks (Article 7), so the first knight is given no added title or adornment.
From the day the first knight is entered, the population in [For anyone introducing Nusquama](PRESS.en.md) is 1.

## When a knight withdraws

The row of a knight who withdraws is not erased. The Knight cell becomes 「辞退（date of withdrawal）」, and the Mistake corrected and Application cells are emptied.
The number and the day of knighting stay. The number is not given to another knight.
The gazette issue keeps its number, with the name taken off ("Knight no. 1 is knighted (the knight later withdrew, and the name was taken off)").
A knight who withdrew is not counted in the population from the year of the withdrawal.
