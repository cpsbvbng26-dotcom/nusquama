Statement of Purpose

Last updated: 2026-11-14.

This is a work of fiction, written for fun. Nusquama is not a state and is not related to any real state, royal house, order of knighthood, honour or title. This statement sets out how the individual who runs this fictional country as play thinks about running it.

This is an English translation. The Japanese version governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

Where this statement says "at present", it means as of the date this page was last updated.

Article 1 (Purpose)

  1. Nusquama is a work of fiction that the operator writes as play. It is an imaginary country made for entertainment, and it is not academic research.
  2. Its motto is "Errata servo, me ipsum rego" ("Mistakes I keep, and myself I rule"). The King does not erase the King's own mistakes, but records them with dates in the King's errata. The King's errata are the domain of this country (Articles 3 and 5 of the Basic Law).
  3. At present, the operator runs this fiction without charging anyone anything.
  4. In doing so, the operator aims to show, as the play of a fictional country, keeping mistakes instead of erasing them and correcting them in public.

Article 2 (Nature of this statement)

  1. This statement sets out the operator's thinking about how Nusquama is run.
  2. This statement is not a provision of the Terms of Use or of any of the rules. By itself it creates no rights or obligations, for the operator or for readers, applicants for knighting, knights or anyone else.
  3. Where this statement and the Terms of Use or a rule differ, the Terms of Use or the rule applies.
  4. The purpose in Article 1 is what the operator aims for. It is neither a promise nor a guarantee that the purpose will be achieved.
  5. This statement is not legal advice.

Article 3 (A work of fiction)

  1. Nusquama is not a state. It has no territory, people, government, currency, passports or nationality, and it claims no sovereignty and no land (Terms of Use, "What Nusquama is").
  2. It is not a state under international law, seeks no recognition as a state, and enters into no diplomatic relations with any state.
  3. It is not related to any real state, royal house, order of knighthood, honours system, decoration or title, and it neither claims to be one nor replaces one.
  4. "Knight-King Nemo I" is the name of a role the operator plays in the fiction.
  5. At present, every page says that it is a work of fiction.

Article 4 (Titles)

  1. The titles of Nusquama (Knight-King, knight, member of the Ordo Erratorum) are words inside the fiction. They are not titles set by the law of any country, and they carry no legal effect, status or privilege (Terms of Use, "Titles").
  2. Knighting does not confer a decoration, honour or rank of knighthood of any country. It is putting the account name of a person who joined the play on the roll.
  3. The titles are used only within this country (Article 8 of the Basic Law).
  4. Nusquama makes and hands out no objects, such as decorations, insignia, uniforms or certificates.

Article 5 (Free of charge)

  1. At present, Nusquama charges no one anything. It sells no titles. At present, it takes no donations, shows no advertising and sells nothing (Terms of Use, "Fees").
  2. At present, applying for knighting and being listed on the roll are free. Knighting is never given in exchange for money, goods or services (Article 6 of the Basic Law).
  3. Anyone who claims to grant Nusquama's titles for a fee has nothing to do with Nusquama.
  4. Being free does not mean any promise that the content is free of errors or that it can always be read. The site and the repository are provided as they are (Terms of Use, "No warranty").

Article 6 (The record of mistakes, and transparency)

  1. When a sentence of the rules is replaced or removed, the sentence before the change is also kept in the King's errata.
  2. The events of the kingdom are published in the timeline on the top page and in the Gazette, each with an issue number.
  3. Whether the promises in the rules are still there is checked by the check code in the repository (Institutions, "Bodies").
  4. Earlier rules can be read in the history of the public repository (Institutions, "Versions").

Article 7 (No ill intent, and obeying the law)

  1. Nusquama means no harm. It does not aim to deceive anyone or to hurt anyone.
  2. The operator runs Nusquama in compliance with the laws of Japan and with the terms of the services it uses.
  3. If the operator learns that something may break a law, the operator stops that part first, then checks it and fixes it. When it is unclear whether something is lawful, the operator chooses not to do it (Terms of Use, "No ill intent, and obeying the law").
  4. The Terms of Use are governed by Japanese law (Terms of Use, "Governing law and disputes").
  5. The reasons the operator believes Nusquama breaks no law are set out, law by law, in the Legality Statement. The Legality Statement is not part of the terms; it explains the operator's understanding.

Article 8 (Knighting, and the countries and regions where knighting is not given)

  1. At present, applications for knighting are not received; they have not been received since 2026-11-12. At present, no date for reopening has been set (Rules on Knighting, "Pause in receiving applications").
  2. From 2026-11-22, only people who live in Japan and are in Japan when they apply may apply (Rules on Knighting, "Who may apply").
  3. From 2026-11-22, people who hold the nationality of a country that the Rules on Knighting exclude by nationality may not apply, even if they live in Japan, and even if they also hold another nationality. At present the countries excluded by nationality number 19: Italy, and the 18 countries in the table under "Countries that restrict foreign titles" in the Rules on Knighting. Beyond the countries in the table, a person whose country of nationality has a law of any of the three types in that section may not apply either.
  4. In addition, from 2026-11-29, people who hold the nationality of, live in, or are in when applying, a country or region where the operator has judged that knighting would not conform to the law of that country or region may not apply either, and are not knighted. When the operator so judges, the country or region and the reason are added to the Rules on Knighting (Rules on Knighting, "Countries and regions where knighting would not conform to the law").
  5. The number of countries and regions where knighting is not given may grow as laws change or as the operator judges.
  6. The purpose of this statement does not change any of these rules.

Article 9 (Rights)

  1. The text and the emblem of Nusquama are published under CC BY 4.0 (Names and rights). Third-party texts quoted or stored for verification are not under CC BY 4.0 and remain their owners'.
  2. CC BY 4.0 neither gives effect to the titles of Nusquama nor allows the emblem to be used as a real insignia.
  3. Reports of an infringement of rights, and requests for removal, correction or withdrawal, are received as set out in Requests.

Article 10 (Personal data)

  1. Personal data is handled as set out in Privacy.
  2. Real names, addresses, affiliations and contact details are not asked for.
  3. Applications for knighting and reports of mistakes are received as public issues on GitHub (United States).
  4. A person under 16 may not apply for knighting.

Article 11 (Use of language models)

  1. Nusquama's text, emblem and site were written with Claude Code (Anthropic) at the operator's request. The operator checked them and is responsible for them (Disclaimer, "How this was made").
  2. Claude is neither a knight nor a king, and is not an author of Nusquama.
  3. At present, Nusquama is not affiliated with Anthropic and is not endorsed by it.

Article 12 (The operator, and relations with others)

  1. At present, the operator is the individual who owns the GitHub account cpsbvbng26-dotcom, and who plays the King.
  2. Nusquama is not a state, government, local authority, royal house, noble family, order of knighthood, religious body, or system of decorations or honours (Disclaimer, "A work of fiction").
  3. Nusquama is separate from the operator's research and is not part of it. It has no relation to the operator's other activities either (Disclaimer, "The operator's research" and "Statements outside the kingdom").
  4. A name on the roll does not mean the operator recommends that person's correction, research or character.
  5. An article on a micronation wiki does not mean recognition or registration (README, section "ほかの場所", Other places).

Article 13 (Responding to disputes)

  1. At present, the operator has not retained a lawyer or any other professional. At present, this statement, the Terms of Use and the rules have not been reviewed by a lawyer.
  2. If a dispute arises, the operator will respond after consulting a lawyer or other professional as needed.
  3. The response follows the laws of Japan (including their provisions on international jurisdiction and on the governing law) and the Terms of Use, "Governing law and disputes". The Terms of Use are governed by Japanese law, and disputes are to be settled by agreement first.
  4. This article is not a warning to anyone.

Article 14 (Continuing and ending)

  1. The operator does not promise that Nusquama will continue (Disclaimer, "Accuracy and availability").
  2. Nusquama may end without notice. If it ends, the roll of knights stays in the repository unless a knight asks to withdraw (Terms of Use, "Changes and ending").
  3. The steps for freezing and dissolution are in Institutions, "Freezing" and "Dissolution".

Article 15 (Changes to this statement)

  1. The operator may change this statement.
  2. When it is changed, the date at the top of this page is updated and a row is added to the timeline on the top page. The rows of the timeline are published in the Gazette.
  3. When the Japanese version is changed, the English translation is changed on the same day.
  4. Changing this statement changes no promise in the Terms of Use or the rules.
