How the symbols are drawn

This is an English translation. The Japanese version governs.

The construction of the flag and the emblem, step by step, with the colours of the kingdom and the shapes of the parcels on the map. The steps are taken from Symbols, and each drawing is made from the elements of the flag image or the emblem image, not drawn by hand. Where they differ, Symbols and the images apply.

The flag

  1. 1. Fill the field with navy.

  2. 2. Draw a gold border 8 thick, 24 in from the edge.

  3. 3. Draw a golden rectangle 534 wide and 330 high in light-gold lines 6 thick, with its top-left corner at (183, 155).

  4. 4. Cut squares out of the rectangle one by one. Their sides shrink by the golden ratio: 330, 204, 126, 77.9, 48.1, 29.8.

  5. 5. Above the rectangle, set three gold squares (the cornerstones) with sides of 24, 12 apart, their top edge at 99.

The emblem

  1. 1. Draw a gold shield and lay a navy shield inside it. A gold border 20 thick is left at the top and the sides.

  2. 2. In the upper half of the shield, draw a golden rectangle 360 wide and 222.5 high, divided into squares, in light-gold lines 4 thick, with its top-left corner at (120, 112). The sides of the squares shrink by the golden ratio: 222.5, 137.5, 85, 52.5, 32.5, 20.1.

  3. 3. At the top of the shield, set three gold squares (the cornerstones) with sides of 18, 10 apart, their top edge at 76.

  4. 4. Draw the sword point down. The pommel and the guard are gold, the grip and the blade light gold, and the groove of the blade deep navy. The point reaches 632.
