The domain, year by year

This is an English translation. The Japanese version governs.

The maps of the domain at the end of each year, side by side. Each map is drawn at the same scale as the current map, so the growth of the domain can be compared. The maps and the numbers are made from the King's errata, not by hand.

One parcel is added each time the King admits a mistake (Article 3 of the Basic Law). The shapes of the parcels are explained in Symbols and the construction of the symbols.
