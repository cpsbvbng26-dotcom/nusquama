A work of fiction

Nusquama is a work of fiction written for fun.It is not a state, government, local authority, royal house, noble family, order of knighthood, religious body, or system of decorations or honours.It is not a state under international law, seeks no recognition as a state, and claims no country's territory.The founding proclamation, the Basic Law, the ten precepts and the emblem are documents inside the fiction.Nusquama means no harm. It does not aim to deceive anyone or to hurt anyone.The promise of no ill intent and of obeying the law is made in the Terms of Use, under "No ill intent, and obeying the law".

Titles have no effect

Names such as Knight-King, knight and member of the Order of Errata are words inside the fiction.No country's law sets them, and they carry no legal effect, status, privileges or honour.Knighting is not the award of a real honour.Having a name on the roll does not show that the person holds any qualification or position.

Relation to real things

Nusquama has no relation to any of the following.

  • Real states, royal houses, governments and local authorities
  • Real orders of knighthood, religious military orders, decorations, and systems of honours (including the Japanese honours system and the British honours system)
  • Every so-called micronation (self-declared nation) other than Nusquama
  • Novels, films, games, animation and other works based on the Arthurian legends, and their rights holders
  • Anyone who publishes or adapts Thomas More's Utopia or Jules Verne's Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas

Where the words "Nusquama", "Nemo" and "Mukau" come from is written on the Names and Rights page (in Japanese). None of them shows a tie to any particular work or organisation.

Applications for knighting

Applications for knighting are received as public issues.The operator does not vouch that the correction described in an application is right.A name on the roll does not mean the operator recommends that person's correction, research or character.
