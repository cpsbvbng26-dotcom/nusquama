A work of fiction

Nusquama is a work of fiction written for fun. It is not a state, government, local authority, royal house, noble family, order of knighthood, religious body, or system of decorations or honours. It is not a state under international law, seeks no recognition as a state, and claims no country's territory. The Founding Declaration, the Basic Law, the Ten Precepts and the emblem are documents inside the fiction. Nusquama means no harm. It does not aim to deceive anyone or to hurt anyone. The promise of no ill intent and of obeying the law is made in the Terms of Use, under "No ill intent, and obeying the law".

Titles have no effect

Names such as Knight-King, knight and member of the Order of Errata are words inside the fiction. No country's law sets them, and they carry no legal effect, status, privileges or honour. Knighting is not the award of a real honour. Having a name on the roll does not show that the person holds any qualification or position.

Relation to real things

Nusquama has no relation to any of the following.

  • Real states, royal houses, governments and local authorities
  • Real orders of knighthood, religious military orders, decorations, and systems of honours (including the Japanese honours system and the British honours system)
  • Every so-called micronation (self-declared nation) other than Nusquama
  • Novels, films, games, animation and other works based on the Arthurian legends, and their rights holders
  • Anyone who publishes or adapts Thomas More's Utopia or Jules Verne's Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas
  • Any music group, work, organisation, person or product that uses the same name as Nusquama or Nemo, or a similar one

Statements outside the kingdom

Inside the kingdom means only this repository, the site, and the articles and posts listed in the README under "ほかの場所", as the Terms of Use set out under the handling of titles. A post or statement not listed there is outside the kingdom, even if the operator wrote it. The rules of Nusquama do not reach it, and what is said there is not a promise of Nusquama. Nusquama has no relation to the operator's other activities either. The titles of Nusquama are not used in those activities, and Nusquama does not recommend them. Nusquama has no plan to obtain a domain of its own. Its only site is https://nusquama.vercel.app/. A site under another domain that calls itself Nusquama is outside the kingdom and has no relation to the operator.

Where the words "Nusquama", "Nemo" and "Mukau" come from is written in Names and rights. None of them shows a tie to any particular work or organisation.

The operator's research

Nusquama is separate from the operator's research and is not part of it. Nusquama is never presented as a research output. The pages of Nusquama do not link to the operator's research pages, and the research pages do not link to Nusquama.

Applications for knighting

Applications for knighting are received as public issues. The operator does not vouch that the correction described in an application is right. A name on the roll does not mean the operator recommends that person's correction, research or character.

The site and the repository are provided as they are. The operator does not promise that they are free of errors, can always be read, or will continue. The site is served by Vercel, and the repository and issues are kept by GitHub. If those services stop, Nusquama can no longer be read. GitHub and Vercel each provide their services "as is", without warranty, and limit their liability under their own terms; Nusquama cannot promise more about their services than they do. The site is served on Vercel's free Hobby plan, on which Vercel may disable or remove a deployment with or without notice. What their terms provide is set out in the Terms of Use, under "GitHub's and Vercel's terms". GitHub's terms let GitHub restrict the use of GitHub Actions, and Actions can stop. While it does not run, none of these runs by itself: the automatic check of applications for knighthood, the closing of applications while the reception is paused, the weekly watch, and the issue of the yearly review. The operator does them by hand, and not at once. Taking an entry down before a decision has always been done by the operator's hand, and does not depend on GitHub Actions.
