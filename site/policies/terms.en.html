What Nusquama is

Nusquama is a work of fiction that the operator writes for fun.It is not a state. It has no territory, people, government, currency, passports or nationality, and it claims no sovereignty and no land.It is not related to any real state, royal house, order of knighthood, honours system, decoration or title, and it neither claims to be one nor replaces one.It issues no currency, points, vouchers, electronic value, passports, identity documents or certificates."Knight-King Nemo I" is the name of a role the operator plays in the fiction.

Nusquama means no harm. Titles

Nusquama's titles (Knight-King, knight, member of the Order of Errata) are words inside a work of fiction.None of them is a title set by the law of any country, and none carries legal effect, status or privileges.The titles are used only inside the kingdom.Inside the kingdom means this repository, the site, and the articles the operator wrote to introduce Nusquama (listed in the README, in Japanese, under "ほかの場所").Nusquama makes and hands out no physical decorations, insignia, uniforms or certificates.The titles must not be used in a way that could be mistaken for an official post, court rank, decoration, academic degree or any other title set by law.Nusquama's titles must not be written on a CV, a paper, a business card, an official document, in commerce or in advertising.

Fees

Nusquama charges no one anything.It sells no titles, takes no donations, shows no advertising and sells nothing.Applying for knighting and being listed on the roll are free.Anyone who claims to grant Nusquama's titles for a fee has nothing to do with Nusquama.

What is not allowed

  • Applying for knighting with someone else's error or with a correction that is not your own.
  • Writing another person's personal information (real name, address, contact details, photographs and so on) in an issue.
  • Defaming, threatening or harassing anyone.
  • Using Nusquama's titles or emblem as if they were a real title, decoration or the insignia of a real organisation.
  • Collecting money or goods in Nusquama's name.
  • Trying to break the site or the forms.

The operator may refuse such applications, close the issues, and remove names from the roll.
