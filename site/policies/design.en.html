About this page

This page records, for each visual part of the site, who made it, where it came from, what rights and licence apply to it, and which articles of which laws it touches. The parts are the typefaces, the emblem, the flag, the favicons, the icons and the image shown when a page is shared, the map of the domain and the figures inside pages, and the colours and the layout. It sets out the operator's own understanding. It is not legal advice, and no lawyer has reviewed it. The articles are quoted as they stand in the copy in verification/statutes-texts.json in this repository (commit 85dd680, fetched from the e-Gov law API on 2026-11-22). The weekly watch fetched and recorded that copy. The English descriptions of the articles on this page are summaries, not translations. Each article is quoted in the Japanese original. The trade mark handling of the names, the flag and the emblem, the search record, and the steps when a rights holder writes are in Names and rights. This page does not repeat them; it links there. The owner asked for this page on 2026-11-22, and Claude wrote it.

Typefaces

The site serves no font file. The style has no rule that loads a font file (font-face), and loads no typeface from outside. The style sets typefaces in 2 places. Each only names, in order, typefaces that may be on the reader's device. If none of the named typefaces is on the device, the generic typeface (serif, ui-monospace or monospace) is used. The typefaces named are on the reader's device and belong to their owners. The emblem, the flag and the map of the domain use no typeface ("Typeface" in Symbols).

Figures inside pages

The figures inside pages are drawn by build.js when it makes the pages of the errata, the domain year by year, and the construction of the symbols. Every figure is made only of rectangles and polygons in the colours of the kingdom, and draws no letters. Every figure has a title.

Colours and layout

The colours and the layout are set by one style in build.js. The style is put into a <style> on each page, and no outside stylesheet is read. No page loads anything from another server (default-src 'none' in the CSP).

Designs

In summary, Article 2(1) of the Design Act defines a design as the shape and similar features of an article or a building, or an image, that gives an aesthetic impression through the eye. Under that paragraph, an image counts only if it is used to operate a device, or is shown as the result of a device performing its function. In summary, Article 23 of the same Act gives the holder of a design right the exclusive right to work the registered design, and designs similar to it, as a business. No record of an application for design registration of the emblem, the flag, the icons or the form of the pages exists in this repository. No record of a comparison with registered designs of others exists either. It was not checked. Nusquama sells nothing, and makes and hands out no objects (Terms of Use).

Accessibility

What the site does

Each of the following is checked by verification/check.js or by a separate job in CI.

  • Colours: a light and a dark colour scheme, switched by the device setting. In both schemes, every pair of text and background is at least 4.5:1, and every pair of focus ring and background is at least 3:1. Every colour is taken from the variables of the two schemes.
  • Not relying on colour alone: the parcels of the map of the domain are all gold, and the categories of mistakes are told apart by shape. Each category has a different shape.
  • Motion: the style has no motion (animation or transition).
  • Text alternatives: the emblem at the top of the page is decoration and has an empty alternative; each image in the content says what it is and adds "(fiction)". Each figure inside a page has a title.
  • Language and headings: every page states its language, has one h1, and skips no heading level.
  • Keyboard: every page starts with a "Skip to content" link. The style draws a ring where the focus is.
  • Zoom: zoom is not blocked. A separate job in CI draws every page in a browser at widths of 360 px and 640 px (the latter matching 200%), and checks that nothing overflows sideways.

Listening with a screen reader, operating the whole site with a keyboard only, and how the site looks for each type of colour vision were not checked.

People and organisations

The SVG images and the figures inside pages are made only of rectangles and polygons. The PNG and ICO images are drawings of the emblem. No image shows a portrait or a photograph of a real person. That the flag and the emblem do not copy the arms or insignia of any real state, royal house, order of knighthood, decoration, university or company is in "The emblem" and "The flag and the emblem" in Names and rights. That the site serves no logo of anyone else is in "Others' names" on the same page.

When something changes

When a part is added, changed or removed, this page and the checks are corrected in the same PR. The checks fail if the list of parts on this page and the images served differ. The change is recorded in the timeline and in the Gazette.
