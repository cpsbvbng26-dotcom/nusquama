# Play

> This is a work of fiction, written for fun. The template and the questions on this page are play within the fiction. Answering them earns nothing.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](PLAY.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

## A template for your own errata

A template for putting errata in your own document.
The table of this template may be used freely without attribution (it is offered under CC0 1.0).
There is no need to mention Nusquama or show its emblem.

| No. | Recorded | Where | Wrong | Right | Checked against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2026-01-01 | Section 2 | (the wrong text, as it was) | (the right text, and how it was changed) | (the source checked; if none, say so) |

Three rules go with it.

1. Do not erase a row. When something is changed, keep the text before the change.
2. Give a date.
3. Say so when something has not been checked.

A mistake corrected in such a table can be used in a request for knighthood ([Rules on Knighting](policies/knighting.en.md)).

## Practice: find the mistake

Each of these five sentences contains one mistake. The answers are under "Answers to the practice" below.

1. A week has eight days.
2. At a pressure of one atmosphere, water boils at about 50 °C.
3. The angles of a triangle in a plane add up to 360 degrees.
4. There are no speling mistakes in this sentence.
5. The knights of Nusquama have three ranks.

## Answers to the practice

1. A week has seven days. Category: Number.
2. About 100 °C. Category: Fact.
3. 180 degrees. Category: Number.
4. "Spelling". Category: Typo.
5. There is only one rank, knight (Article 7 of the Basic Law). Category: Fact.

## Answers to the seasonal questions

### Founding Day (7 November)

1. Nemo I, Knight-King (Founding Declaration).
2. Errata servo, me ipsum rego. ("Mistakes I keep, and myself I rule.")
3. The King compares the ten-year outlook with what happened and announces the result in the gazette ([Institutions](INSTITUTIONS.en.md), "Ten years ahead").
4. The issue for the yearly review ([Institutions](INSTITUTIONS.en.md), "The yearly review").

### Day of Errata (8 November)

1. Where the kingdom's name came from ([Story of the founding](STORY.en.md)).
2. In errata: one entry in the King's errata is one erratum ([Symbols](SYMBOLS.en.md), "Time and units").
3. It is not erased. A new row records that the earlier row was wrong ([Institutions](INSTITUTIONS.en.md), "Pardon").
4. It grows by one parcel ([Institutions](INSTITUTIONS.en.md), "Pardon").
