This page is part of the [Terms of Use](./terms.en.md). Where this page and the main text of the Terms differ, the main text of the Terms applies.

## A work of fiction

Nusquama is a work of fiction written for fun.
It is not a state, government, local authority, royal house, noble family, order of knighthood, religious body, or system of decorations or honours.
It is not a state under international law, seeks no recognition as a state, and claims no country's territory.
The founding proclamation, the Basic Law, the ten precepts and the emblem are documents inside the fiction.
Nusquama means no harm. It does not aim to deceive anyone or to hurt anyone.
The promise of no ill intent and of obeying the law is made in the Terms of Use, under "No ill intent, and obeying the law".

## Titles have no effect

Names such as Knight-King, knight and member of the Order of Errata are words inside the fiction.
No country's law sets them, and they carry no legal effect, status, privileges or honour.
Knighting is not the award of a real honour.
Having a name on the roll does not show that the person holds any qualification or position.

## Relation to real things

Nusquama has no relation to any of the following.

- Real states, royal houses, governments and local authorities
- Real orders of knighthood, religious military orders, decorations, and systems of honours (including the Japanese honours system and the British honours system)
- Every so-called micronation (self-declared nation) other than Nusquama
- Novels, films, games, animation and other works based on the Arthurian legends, and their rights holders
- Anyone who publishes or adapts Thomas More's Utopia or Jules Verne's Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas
- Any music group, work, organisation, person or product that uses the same name as Nusquama or Nemo, or a similar one

Inside the kingdom means only this repository, the site, and the articles and posts listed in the README under "ほかの場所", as the Terms of Use set out under the handling of titles.
A post or statement not listed there is outside the kingdom, even if the operator wrote it.
The rules of Nusquama do not reach it, and what is said there is not a promise of Nusquama.
Nusquama has no relation to the operator's other activities either.
The titles of Nusquama are not used in those activities, and Nusquama does not recommend them.

Where the words "Nusquama", "Nemo" and "Mukau" come from is written on the Names and Rights page (in Japanese). None of them shows a tie to any particular work or organisation.
