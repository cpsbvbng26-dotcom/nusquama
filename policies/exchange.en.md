# Rules on exchange

Last updated: 2026-11-11.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](./exchange.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

## About these rules

These rules set out how Nusquama deals with other micronations (self-declared states) and fictional countries.
Nusquama is a work of fiction, not a state. Exchange, too, takes place within the fiction.

## How exchange takes place

- Exchange is limited to greetings and links to each other's pages.
- No treaty, alliance or document of mutual recognition is concluded, so that no promise looks like a relation between states.
- No title, decoration or rank is conferred on each other. The only thing Nusquama gives is knighthood under the Rules on Knighting.
- No exchange involves money, goods or services.
- There is no exchange with anything that claims to be a real state, royal house or order of knighthood, that sells titles, or that deceives people.
- The other party, too, is respected as a work of its own, and Nusquama neither accepts nor denies the claims of its country.
- Besides titles, there is no exchange with anything that sells citizenship, passports, land or ranks.
- Nusquama appoints no ambassadors or consuls, and receives none from the other party.

## When an offer is received

An offer of exchange is received where it arrives and answered in the same place. There is no form for offers.
Before answering, the King checks the following.

1. Whether the other party says somewhere that it is a self-declared or fictional country. If no such statement can be found, the King asks.
2. Whether the other party sells titles, citizenship, passports, land or ranks.
3. Whether the offer is limited to greetings and links to each other's pages.
4. Whether the offer involves money, goods or services.

If a treaty, an alliance, recognition or an exchange of ambassadors is asked for, the King declines it, and answers that greetings and links alone are welcome.
An offer that is declined is still answered. The reason given is only which of these rules applies.

## What the other party writes

If the other party writes as if there were a treaty, an alliance or recognition between it and Nusquama, the King asks for a correction. If it is not corrected, the exchange ends.
If the other party writes that it has given a title or decoration to the King or to a knight of Nusquama, the King answers that it is not accepted, and asks for the statement to be taken down.

## How it is decided and ended

The King decides whether to engage in an exchange.
When an exchange begins, a link to the other party's page is put, with the date, under "Elsewhere" on the top page.
The day it begins is also kept in the timeline.
Either side may end an exchange at any time. When it ends, the link is taken down, and the day is kept in the timeline.

## Requests

The operator of the other country may ask for a link to be corrected or taken down, by the procedure in [Requests for removal, correction or withdrawal](./requests.en.md).
