These terms cover everyone who reads Nusquama's site and repository, everyone who applies for knighting, and everyone whose name is on the roll of knights.

Reading the site or the repository is not taken as agreement to anything; for readers, these terms describe how Nusquama works, and the law decides what each side owes.
The agreement on courts is made only with those who agreed in the application form for knighting ("Governing law and disputes").
The [Disclaimer](./disclaimer.en.md), [Privacy](./privacy.en.md), the [Rules on Knighting](./knighting.en.md), the [Requests for removal, correction or withdrawal](./requests.en.md) and [Names and rights](./names.en.md) are also part of these terms.
The reasons the operator believes Nusquama breaks no law are set out, law by law, in the [Legality Statement](./legality.en.md). The Legality Statement is not part of these terms; it explains the operator's understanding.

## What Nusquama is

Nusquama is a work of fiction that the operator writes for fun.
It is not a state. It has no territory, people, government, currency, passports or nationality, and it claims no sovereignty and no land.
It is not a state under international law, seeks no recognition as a state, and enters into no diplomatic relations with any state.
It is not related to any real state, royal house, order of knighthood, honours system, decoration or title, and it neither claims to be one nor replaces one.
It issues no currency, points, vouchers, electronic value, passports, identity documents or certificates.
"Knight-King Nemo I" is the name of a role the operator plays in the fiction.

## No ill intent, and obeying the law

Nusquama means no harm. It does not aim to deceive anyone or to hurt anyone.
The operator runs Nusquama in compliance with the laws of Japan and with the terms of the services it uses.
If the operator learns that something may break a law, the operator stops that part first, then checks it and fixes it.
When it is unclear whether something is lawful, the operator chooses not to do it.

## Titles

Nusquama's titles (Knight-King, knight, member of the Order of Errata) are words inside a work of fiction.
None of them is a title set by the law of any country, and none carries legal effect, status or privileges.
Knighting does not confer a decoration, honour or rank of knighthood (including a rank in an order) of any country; it records on the roll the account name of a person who joined the game. The name of knight is a role name inside the fiction that goes with that entry.
The titles are used only inside the kingdom.
Inside the kingdom means this repository, the site, and the articles and posts the operator wrote to introduce Nusquama (listed in the [README](../README.md), in Japanese, under "ほかの場所").
Nusquama makes and hands out no physical decorations, insignia, uniforms or certificates.
The titles must not be used in a way that could be mistaken for an official post, court rank, decoration, academic degree or any other title set by law.
Nusquama's titles must not be written on a CV, a paper, a business card, an official document, in commerce or in advertising.
From 2026-11-22, the name of knight is used only in connection with Nusquama.
From 2026-11-22, the name of knight is never presented as a title of nobility, an honour, an order, a rank or a dignity of any state, and is not used in an official, commercial or legal context.
From 2026-11-22, if this is breached, the operator removes the name from the roll ([Rules on Knighting](./knighting.en.md), "Use of the name of knight").

## Fees

Nusquama charges no one anything.
It sells no titles, takes no donations, shows no advertising and sells nothing.
Applying for knighting and being listed on the roll are free.
Anyone who claims to grant Nusquama's titles for a fee has nothing to do with Nusquama.

## What is not allowed

- Applying for knighting with someone else's error or with a correction that is not your own.
- Writing another person's personal information (real name, address, contact details, photographs and so on) in an issue.
- Defaming, threatening or harassing anyone.
- Using Nusquama's titles or emblem as if they were a real title, decoration or the insignia of a real organisation.
- Collecting money or goods in Nusquama's name.
- Trying to break the site or the forms.
- Ticking a box in the application form for something that is not true, such as your age, the country you live in, your nationality, or that the correction is of your own error.

The operator may refuse such applications, close the issues, and remove names from the roll.
If a ticked box turns out to be untrue, the operator removes the name from the roll as soon as the operator learns of it. From 2026-11-22, the operator may also refuse further applications from the same GitHub account.
The operator relies on the ticked boxes in good faith and does not check them. A person knighted on an untrue box does not mean that Nusquama accepts applications from a country, or from holders of a nationality, from which it says it does not.
The ticked boxes stay in the public issue until it is deleted at the person's request, so that what was ticked, and when, can be shown.
Nothing here asks an applicant for a penalty, or for damages beyond what the law provides.

## Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by Japanese law.
Nothing in these terms limits a right that the law does not allow to be limited.
Disputes are to be settled by agreement first.
Before bringing any claim, the operator first talks with the other party.
The talks take place in an issue. When the other party has made a contact public on their own GitHub profile, they may also take place at that contact ([Requests for removal, correction or withdrawal](./requests.en.md), "Requests that cannot be made in public").
If the other party offers to settle, the operator responds in good faith.
A dispute not settled by agreement goes, according to the amount in dispute, to the Utsunomiya District Court or the Utsunomiya Summary Court, as the court of first instance by agreement, for a person who has agreed to these terms in the application form for knighting, and only for suits based on these terms or on that person's own application for knighting, listing on the roll of knights or withdrawal.
This agreement is not exclusive.
Any other dispute, and any dispute with a person who has not agreed in the form, goes to the court that the law sets.
When the operator brings a suit against a person these terms cover, the operator brings it only in the Utsunomiya District Court or the Utsunomiya Summary Court, or in the court with jurisdiction over the place of that person's general forum.
When the other party brings a suit in the Utsunomiya District Court or the Utsunomiya Summary Court, the operator does not contest jurisdiction, whatever the dispute.
This does not take away the right of a consumer, or of a person outside Japan, to sue in another court where the law allows it.
