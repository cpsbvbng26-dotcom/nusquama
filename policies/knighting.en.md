# Rules on Knighting

Last updated: 2026-11-26.

This is an English translation. The [Japanese version](./knighting.md) governs; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

## What knighting is

Knighting is giving the name of knight, within a work of fiction.
It brings no legal effect, status, privilege or honour.
Knighting does not confer a decoration, honour or rank of knighthood of any country.
Knighting is free. It is never given in exchange for money, goods or services (Basic Law, Article 6).
Knighting issues no document or object, such as a letter of appointment, a certificate or an insignia. It only puts an account name on the roll.
Knighting is done entirely online. Applications are received as GitHub issues, and a knighting is recorded as a comment on that issue and on the roll of knights in the repository and on the site.
There is no knighting ceremony and no conferral in person. No letter or object is sent either.
What Nusquama gives to people is only the name of knight. No other title, rank, decoration, insignia, certificate or nationality is given, under whatever name or form.

Article 6 of the Basic Law of the Kingdom sets one condition for knighting: to have publicly corrected one of one's own errors.
The conditions of these rules apply in addition to the condition of Article 6.
Where these rules add a condition, these rules prevail.
The added conditions are age, the country of residence, the country where the applicant is when applying, nationality, the premise of good sense, and the pause in receiving applications.
From 2026-11-24, the condition in "Never having been outside Japan" is also one of the added conditions.
From 2026-12-15, the condition in "Holding Japanese nationality" is also one of the added conditions.
After applications reopen, the condition in "Checking Japanese nationality by document" is also one of the added conditions.
From 2026-11-29, the rule in "Countries and regions where knighting would not conform to the law" is also one of the added conditions.
From 2026-12-15, the conditions in "Barriers to participation" are also among the added conditions.
From 2026-12-15, the conditions in "Barriers to participation (continued)" are also among the added conditions.
A person who meets the condition of Article 6 but not the conditions of these rules is not knighted.

## Who may apply

A person who has publicly corrected one of their own mistakes.
The correction must be public and checkable by anyone, such as an erratum to a paper, a note added to a blog post or article, a fix to code, or a statement withdrawn.
A person under 16 may not apply.
A person whom the law of the country where they live forbids to receive or use a title of knighthood given by a private person may not apply either. For example, Article 8 of Italy's Law No. 178 of 1951 forbids private persons and bodies to confer titles of knighthood under any name.
From 2026-11-22, only people who live in Japan and are in Japan when they apply may apply. Nationality does not matter, except that people who hold Italian nationality, or the nationality of a country under "Countries that restrict foreign titles", are excluded.
From 2026-11-22, people who live outside Japan may not apply, whatever their nationality, because the laws outside Japan cannot all be checked country by country.
From 2026-11-22, a person who lives in Japan may not apply while outside Japan, for travel, business or study, because some foreign laws reach not only those who live there but those who are there.
From 2026-11-22, a person who holds Italian nationality may not apply, even if they live in Japan, and even if they also hold another nationality. There are two reasons.
First, by plain common sense, an Italian national becoming a knight of a Japanese micronation is out of place to begin with.
Second, Article 8 of Italy's Law No. 178 of 1951 also punishes anyone who uses such a title, and the penalty for use applies even when the title was conferred abroad. The place the last paragraph of the article names as abroad is only the place of conferral ("quando il conferimento delle onorificenze, decorazioni o distinzioni sia avvenuto all'estero"). It says nothing of the place where the title is used. Whether that penalty reaches an Italian national who uses the title outside Italy is not settled by the fetched texts.
Excluding people who hold Italian nationality is the operator's own rule. It only excludes applicants, and says nothing about how far the article reaches.
Whether the article's penalty for conferring reaches the operator is not settled by the fetched texts. For that reason, the operator keeps applications paused ("Pause in receiving applications"; [Legality Statement](./legality.en.md), "Laws outside Japan").
A person who only stays in Japan briefly, such as a traveller, does not count as living in Japan.
From 2026-11-24, only people who have never been outside Japan since birth may apply ("Never having been outside Japan").
From 2026-11-29, people who hold the nationality of, live in, or are in when applying, a country or region where the operator has judged that knighting would not conform to the law of that country or region may not apply either ("Countries and regions where knighting would not conform to the law").
From 2026-12-15, a person under 18 may not apply ("Barriers to participation").
From 2026-12-15, the correction must fix an error in a text the applicant wrote and published, be made in a place that keeps the earlier version, and have been published at least 180 days before the day the application issue was opened ("Barriers to participation (continued)").
From 2026-12-15, applications are received only from GitHub accounts created at least 2 years before the day the issue was opened. No application is ever received again from an account whose application was declined ("Barriers to participation (continued)").
From 2026-12-15, only people who hold Japanese nationality may apply. A person who holds Italian nationality, or the nationality of a country under "Countries that restrict foreign titles", together with Japanese nationality still may not apply ("Holding Japanese nationality").

## Checking the place when applications reopen (the checked intake)

No system can stop a person from lying in general.

This is called the checked intake.
Applications stay paused. This section sets the condition for reopening; it does not decide to reopen.
When applications reopen, living in Japan is accepted not on the applicant's statement but on the following check.
The applicant gives an ORCID iD, and the public ORCID record of that iD must link the GitHub account that opened the application issue.

No nationality is read. The GitHub profile is not read.

The countries and organisations read are not kept, not written, and not printed in the logs; the issue shows only whether the check passed.
This proves that earlier public records agree, not where a person lives: a person who keeps false records for 30 days can still pass. The rule of the Terms of Use against ticking a box untruly, and the removal from the roll, therefore stay as they are.

## The premise of good sense

From 2026-11-22, knighting presumes that the applicant is a person of good sense who treats the title as play.
This premise means using the title only within the fiction, and keeping the following rules.

- Use the title only within the kingdom ([Terms of Use](./terms.en.md), "Titles"; Basic Law, Article 8).
- Do not present the title as if it were a real title, decoration or insignia of an organisation.
- Do not put the title on a CV, a paper, a business card, an official document, a business transaction or an advertisement.
- Do not sell the title, and do not use it to collect money or goods.
- Do not use the title to deceive anyone.

These set out, as ways of using the title, the rules in the Terms of Use ("Titles", "What is not allowed", "No ill intent, and obeying the law") and Article 8 of the Basic Law.
From 2026-11-22, the applicant ticks a box in the form for this premise.
If what the applicant wrote, or the linked correction, shows clearly that this premise does not hold, the applicant is not knighted ("What is declined").
If it becomes clear after knighting, the knighting is revoked under "Withdrawal and revocation".
When revoking, the operator writes in the application issue only which rule was not kept, briefly, and comments on no one. The issue is deleted if the person asks.

### The documents accepted

Only a personal record certificate of the applicant's family register (koseki), or an extract of a paper family register (koseki shohon), is accepted. Both certify part of what is recorded in a family register (Family Register Act, Articles 10 and 120).
It must have been issued within 3 months. A loss of nationality after the day of issue does not appear on it.
Article 23 of the Family Register Act provides that a person who loses nationality is removed from the family register they were in (joseki).
The operator checks that there is no mark of removal in the applicant's entry on the certificate.
A certificate of all matters of a family register, and a full copy of a family register (koseki tohon), are not accepted, because they carry other people's entries.
A certificate of a removed family register (joseki) is not accepted either.
A passport is not accepted. Article 18(1)(i) of the Passport Act provides that a passport loses its effect when its holder loses Japanese nationality. An image of a passport cannot show whether it has lost its effect that way. A passport also carries a photograph of the face.
A copy of a certificate of residence (jūminhyō) is not accepted. Nationality is not among the matters that Article 7 of the Basic Resident Registration Act lists for a certificate of residence. Nationality and region are recorded on the certificate of residence of a foreign resident (Article 30-45 of the same Act). Article 7 of the same Act also lists the Individual Number among the matters recorded.
An Individual Number Card (My Number card) is not accepted. Nationality is not among the matters that Article 2(7) of the Number Use Act lists for the card. The card carries the Individual Number.
No other document is accepted.

### The Individual Number is not received

Article 19 of the Number Use Act (Act on the Use of Numbers to Identify a Specific Individual in Administrative Procedures) provides that no person may provide specific personal information except in the cases listed in its items.
Article 20 of the same Act provides that no person may collect or keep specific personal information that includes another person's Individual Number, except in the same cases.
Specific personal information is personal information that contains an Individual Number (Article 2(9) of the same Act).
The operator relies on none of the items of Article 19. The operator does not receive, collect or keep any Individual Number.
Neither side of an Individual Number Card, a copy of a certificate of residence that shows the Individual Number, nor anything else that shows the Individual Number is put in the image.
If an Individual Number appears in an image, the operator does not read it, deletes the private repository on that day, and records the check as not confirmed.

### What is masked and what is shown

The permanent domicile (honseki), the name of the head of the register, the applicant's name, date of birth, the names of the parents and adoptive parents, the relationship, what is written under the matters of status, and the serial number of the certificate are masked.
Nothing else is masked. The title of the certificate, the statement by which the mayor certifies it, the day of issue, and the mayor's title and name stay visible. If the applicant's entry has a mark of removal, that mark stays visible too.
The name is masked because the check does not need it. The operator does not know the applicant's real name, so seeing the name would not tie it to the account. The rule that no real name is asked for is also kept ([Privacy](./privacy.en.md), "What an application for knighthood involves").
The permanent domicile, the date of birth, the parents and the matters of status are not needed to check Japanese nationality either.
Masking is done by laying paper over the parts or by painting over them on the image.
The image is a single JPEG or PNG. A PDF is not accepted, because text under a painted area can remain in a PDF.
The image keeps no location data of where it was taken.
If a part that should be masked is visible, the operator does not read it and records the check as not confirmed.
If a part that should be shown is hidden and the check cannot be decided, it is also recorded as not confirmed.

### Tying it to the account

In the same image, next to the certificate, a handwritten sheet of paper is shown.
On the sheet are written the GitHub account name that opened the application issue and the day the image is sent (Japan time, in the form 2026-12-15).
If the date on the sheet differs from the day (Japan time) the image is placed in the repository, the check is recorded as not confirmed.
If the day of issue is more than 3 months before the date on the sheet, it is also recorded as not confirmed.

### How the image is passed

The image is received only in a private repository on GitHub. Neither email nor any other service is used.
No contact details are asked for, and none are newly published.
After checking the other conditions, and before deciding on knighting, the operator creates one private repository for each application.
Only the GitHub account that opened the application issue is invited to that repository. The invitation is noted in the application issue.
After accepting the invitation, the applicant places the image in that repository as a file.
The image is not pasted into an issue, a comment or a pull request, because GitHub's notifications may carry a copy of the content of an issue ([Privacy](./privacy.en.md), "How long data is kept").
The operator only looks at the image in GitHub's web interface. The operator does not download, copy or store it.
The operator gives the image neither to a language model nor to GitHub's AI features.
Claude does not see the images. Claude does not create, open or read these private repositories. Only the operator does this check.
The operator deletes the private repository within 7 days of the day it was created. Once the operator has looked at the image, the repository is deleted on that day.
If no image is placed within the 7 days, the check is recorded as not confirmed.
The operator does not restore a deleted repository.

### What is recorded

The operator records only one line, in the public application issue.
That line is "日本の国籍の書類による確かめ：確かめた（date）" (checked) or "日本の国籍の書類による確かめ：確かめられなかった（date）" (not confirmed).
No copy of the certificate, no image, name, date of birth, permanent domicile, certificate number, issuing municipality or day of issue is recorded anywhere.
What the roll of knights carries does not change.
Knighting is not decided until that line is recorded.
An application whose check was not confirmed is declined ("What is declined").

### What this check shows

This check shows only that an image of a certificate matching the declaration was shown next to a sheet with that account name.
It cannot exclude a forged document or another person's certificate.
So the handling of an untrue tick does not change. If a tick turns out to be untrue, or the applicant turns out to have shown a forged document or another person's document, it is handled under "What is not allowed" in the Terms of Use: the operator removes the name from the roll as soon as the operator learns of it, and may also refuse further applications from the same GitHub account.
The content of the change and the date it takes effect were shown on 2026-11-26 under "Changes and ending" in the Terms of Use.

## Countries that restrict foreign titles

From 2026-11-22, a person who holds the nationality of any of the 18 countries in the table below may not apply, even if they live in Japan, and even if they also hold another nationality. Claude decided this on 2026-11-12.
Austria, Greece and Yemen were added to the table by Claude on 2026-11-13: the provisions on titles that a type rule had classed as not applying were read again one by one, and provisions of type 1 or type 3 were found. For Austria, the 1919 law abolishing nobility, which Article 149 of the Constitution treats as constitutional law, was fetched and read, and was read as type 3. People who hold the nationality of a country added are also unable to apply from 2026-11-22. Applications have been paused since 2026-11-12, so no one is refused retroactively.
On the texts fetched and read, the constitution or a law of each country in the table is of one of three types.

1. Type 1: it forbids accepting a foreign title, order, honour or rank, or requires the State's permission to accept one.
2. Type 2: it attaches loss of nationality or citizenship, or a penalty, to accepting a foreign title or the like.
3. Type 3: it punishes assuming a title of nobility that is not one's own, and the name of knight could be read as such a title.

Nusquama is not a state, and the name of knight is not a title of any country.
Even so, the words of these provisions allow a reading on which they apply, so that path is closed as follows.
The strictest reading was taken, and such applications are not accepted. This costs nothing, and not accepting them closes that path.
Some provisions bind only public officials or holders of State office. The operator does not check an applicant's office, so the whole nationality is excluded.

## Countries and regions where knighting would not conform to the law

From 2026-11-29, people who hold the nationality of, live in, or are in when applying, a country or region where the operator has judged that knighting would not conform to the law of that country or region may not apply, and are not knighted.
This rule applies in addition to "Who may apply" and "Countries that restrict foreign titles".
When the operator so judges, the country or region and the reason are added to this section. Such an addition is shown as the Terms of Use, "Changes and ending", provide.
As of 2026-11-13, no country or region has been excluded under this rule.
The owner asked for this on 2026-11-13, and Claude decided it: Article 8 of the [Statement of Purpose](../MISSION.en.md) says that knighting is not given in countries and regions where it would not conform to the law, and since the statement by itself sets no rule, the rule is placed in these rules.

## Use of the name of knight

From 2026-11-22, the name of knight is used only within the following three rules. Claude decided this on 2026-11-12.

- Use it only in connection with Nusquama.
- Never present it as a title of nobility, an honour, an order, a rank or a dignity of any state.
- Do not use it in an official, commercial or legal context (documents, filings, contracts, courts and the like).

If it becomes known that these rules were breached, the name is removed from the roll.
The reason is that the following provisions, fetched and read, punish assuming titles, honours or ranks without the right to them, or using them without permission.

- Germany: Strafgesetzbuch (StGB) § 132a
- Argentina: Código Penal de la Nación (Ley 11.179, t.o. 1984) ARTICULO 247
- Mexico: Código Penal Federal Artículo 250
- Italy: Codice penale (R.D. 19 ottobre 1930, n. 1398) Art. 498 (paragraph 5); Legge 3 marzo 1951, n. 178 (Ordine al merito della Repubblica) Art. 7 (paragraph 2)
- Belgium: Code pénal Art. 230
- Netherlands: Wetboek van Strafrecht Artikel 435
- Norway: Lov om straff (straffeloven) § 165 (only the heading could be taken from the fetched page)
- Portugal: Código Penal (Decreto-Lei n.º 48/95) Artigo 307 (only the heading could be taken from the fetched page)

The texts of the provisions are quoted in the [survey of the world's laws](../docs/world-law-2026-11-12.md) (in Japanese), "2026-11-12 の続き：当てはめ".
Whether use that keeps these three rules falls within what these provisions punish is not settled by the fetched texts. The operator keeps applications paused ("Pause in receiving applications").

## How to apply

Vercel, which serves the site, and GitHub, which keeps the applications, may use what is placed on them to train AI models. If you are worried about your personal data, do not apply. These uses are described in [Privacy](./privacy.en.md).

1. Once applications reopen, open an issue with the form Application for knighthood (while applications are paused, the form is not offered; "Pause in receiving applications").
2. Give a public link to the correction, and say what was wrong and how you corrected it.
3. Tick every one of the pledges.

What an applicant agrees to is the version of the rules on the day of the application. That version can be checked in the history of the public repository.

Do not write your real name, address, affiliation or contact details.

From 2026-12-15, also fill in the field "称号を使わない場所" (Places where the title is not used) in the form, and tick the box that you are 18 or over ("Barriers to participation").

From 2026-12-15, also fill in the field "訂正を公開した日" (Day the correction was published) in the form, and tick the box that the original text with the error is a text you wrote ("Barriers to participation (continued)").

From 2026-12-15, also tick the box that you hold Japanese nationality ("Holding Japanese nationality").

After applications reopen, also tick the box that you accept "Checking Japanese nationality by document", and place an image of your family register certificate in the private repository the operator invites you to ("Checking Japanese nationality by document").

## What the operator checks

- That the link is public and reads as a correction.
- That it reads as a correction of the applicant's own mistake.
- That it contains no personal data of others and nothing that hurts others.
- That every pledge in the form is ticked.
- From 2026-12-15, that at the link the day of publication matches "訂正を公開した日" (Day the correction was published) in the form, that both the original text with the error and the corrected text can be seen, and that the original text reads as written by the applicant (for a co-authored text, one with the applicant among its authors) ("Barriers to participation (continued)").
- That the linked correction does not state clearly that the applicant lives outside Japan, is outside Japan at the time of applying, holds Italian nationality or the nationality of a country under "Countries that restrict foreign titles", or is under 16. If it does, the operator asks the applicant, and does not knight them unless it is confirmed that they meet the conditions.
- From 2026-12-15, that the linked correction does not state clearly that the applicant does not hold Japanese nationality. If it does, the operator asks the applicant, and does not knight them unless it is confirmed that they hold Japanese nationality ("Holding Japanese nationality").
- After applications reopen, that the result of "Checking Japanese nationality by document" is "確かめた" (checked). The operator does not decide on knighting until that line is recorded in the application issue.

The operator does not judge whether the correction itself is right. The operator judges only that a correction was made.

When an application issue is opened or edited, an automatic check looks at the ticks in the form and whether the link to the correction is an https URL that does not point to a refused destination, and comments the result in the issue.
The automatic check does not open the link to the correction. The operator is the one who opens and reads it.
The refused destinations are localhost; names ending in .localhost, .local, .internal, .home.arpa, .test, .example, .invalid or .onion; addresses written as numbers; names without a dot; and URLs that contain a user name or password. None of them points to a public page.
From 2026-11-24, the automatic check also checks the tick that the applicant has never been outside Japan since birth ("Never having been outside Japan").
From 2026-12-15, the automatic check also checks the tick that the applicant is 18 or over, the answer under "称号を使わない場所" (Places where the title is not used) and the dates of the same account's earlier applications, and writes the earliest day on which knighting can be decided ("Barriers to participation").
From 2026-12-15, the automatic check also checks the tick that the original text with the error is a text the applicant wrote, the day the correction was published, whether the link to the correction has the form of a place that keeps the earlier version, the day the account was created, and declined applications from the same account ("Barriers to participation (continued)").
From 2026-12-15, the automatic check also checks the tick that the applicant holds Japanese nationality ("Holding Japanese nationality").
After applications reopen, the automatic check also checks the tick that the applicant accepts "Checking Japanese nationality by document", and writes that the document check is not done until the operator records its result. The automatic check does not see the image.
The automatic check decides nothing. The operator decides on knighting.

## What is not checked

The operator does not check country of residence, age or real name, and asks for no identity document with a photograph of the face.
Nationality is checked only after applications reopen, and only in the form of "Checking Japanese nationality by document".
Nusquama gives no nationality. Knights are not its people.
Age, country of residence, the country where the applicant is when applying, and not holding Italian nationality or the nationality of a country under "Countries that restrict foreign titles" are taken from the applicant's own declaration, by the ticks in the form.
From 2026-11-24, never having been outside Japan since birth is also taken from the applicant's own declaration, by a tick in the form. No passport or other document is asked for ("Never having been outside Japan").
From 2026-12-15, holding Japanese nationality is also taken from the applicant's own declaration, by a tick in the form. After applications reopen, "Checking Japanese nationality by document" is added to it. No passport, certificate of residence or Individual Number Card is asked for ("Holding Japanese nationality").
But what the linked correction clearly states is not overlooked.
The person dealt with is the GitHub account that applied.
If it turns out that an applicant did not meet the conditions, their name is taken off the roll.
If the application was from a person under 16, its issue is also deleted, without being asked.
From 2026-12-15, if the application was from a person under 18, its issue is also deleted, without being asked ("Barriers to participation").

## What is declined

No knighthood is given for:

- Anything that is not a correction of a mistake (advertising, self-introduction, pointing out someone else's mistake, and so on).
- Anything that cannot be checked (something not public, or a link that does not open).
- Anything that falls under "What is not allowed" in the Terms of Use.
- An application where the linked correction clearly shows that the applicant does not meet the conditions (age, country of residence, the country where the applicant is when applying, or nationality).
- An application opened from 2026-11-12 until applications reopen ("Pause in receiving applications").
- From 2026-11-22, an application where what the applicant wrote, or the linked correction, clearly shows that "The premise of good sense" does not hold.
- From 2026-11-24, an application without the tick for "Never having been outside Japan".
- From 2026-12-15, an application that does not meet the conditions in "Barriers to participation".
- An application in which a pledge of the form that must be ticked is not ticked.
- From 2026-11-29, an application where the linked correction clearly shows that "Countries and regions where knighting would not conform to the law" applies.
- From 2026-12-15, an application that does not meet the conditions in "Barriers to participation (continued)".
- From 2026-12-15, an application without the tick for "Holding Japanese nationality".
- After applications reopen, an application whose "Checking Japanese nationality by document" was not confirmed.

When declining, the operator writes only one fixed sentence in the issue ("This does not meet the conditions for knighting."), and comments on no one. The issue is deleted if the applicant asks.
From 2026-12-15, the issue of a declined application gets the label 断った (declined). No application for knighting is ever received again from that account ("Barriers to participation (continued)").
An application closed during the pause, and an application declined only because of 1 person in 2 years, do not count as declined applications here.

## The roll

Those knighted are entered on the [roll of knights](../KNIGHTS.en.md) in the order of their knighting.
On knighting, the operator posts a comment on the application issue with a link to the row of the roll, and closes the issue as completed.
There is only one rank, "knight", with nothing above or below it (Basic Law, Article 7).
The roll gives each knight by account name only. A knight is called "Knight" followed by the account name, with no other title or adornment added.

## Withdrawal and revocation

A knight may ask at any time to be taken off the roll. No reason is needed. On withdrawal, their name is taken off the roll.
The operator may revoke a knighthood when a knight uses the title outside this kingdom ([Terms of Use](./terms.en.md), "Titles"; Basic Law, Article 8) or breaks the Terms of Use. On revocation, the name is taken off the roll.
The kingdom keeps its mistakes, but a person's name is different. A name taken off the roll is not kept even in a record of the revocation, unless the person asks for that.
